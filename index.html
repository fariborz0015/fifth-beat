Four beats. Then silence.

The circle lands on the line four times with light, a click and a tap of haptics. Then it goes quiet. You tap once, exactly when the fifth beat would have landed, and the game tells you how close you were, to the millisecond.

A tiny daily ritual

One thumb. Thirty seconds. Playable muted, in bed, with your eyes closed if you like. Honest feedback on every tap, a chart of your timing after every run, and one tempo per day shared by everyone.

No tricks

No timers. No streak shaming. No currency, no loot boxes. One optional ad for an extra life, and a single purchase removes interstitial ads forever.
