How do I play?

Tap Begin. The circle lands four times. Then it goes silent. Tap anywhere on the screen at the exact moment the fifth beat would land. The number you see is how early or late you were, in milliseconds. Three misses end a run.

The haptics feel off, or I always read as early or late

Open Settings and choose Calibrate my timing. Tap along with five beats and the game learns your personal offset. If your device has haptics turned off in iOS Settings, the game cannot vibrate.

Playing with sound off

The count is carried by haptics, so the game works fully muted. Eyes closed mode in Settings turns the visuals off entirely and keeps only the haptics.

Flashing, motion, or late night play

Settings has Reduce flashing, Reduce motion, a Dawn theme for daylight, and Slow beat for bedtime at 60 BPM with no lives.

Ads and Remove Ads

The first session has no ads. Later, one optional rewarded ad can give an extra life, and a short interstitial may appear after a score card at most once every three runs. Remove Ads is a single purchase in Settings. Restore Purchases is right beside it if you reinstall or change phones. To report an ad that was misleading or inappropriate, email the address above with the time it appeared.

Your data

Fifth Beat has no accounts. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for what the ad network collects.
