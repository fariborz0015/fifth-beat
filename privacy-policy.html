Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. This policy explains what Fifth Beat (the app) collects, what it does not collect, and the choices you have.

In short

Fifth Beat has no accounts, no login, no chat and no server of its own. Your scores, streak and settings are stored only on your device. The only third party in the app is Google AdMob, which serves the optional ads, and Apple, which handles the Remove Ads purchase.

What the app stores on your device

Your best score, run history for the score card, daily streak, timing calibration and settings. This data never leaves your phone unless you back up your device with iCloud or a computer. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small number of ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad for an extra life and an occasional short interstitial after a score card. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), your IP address and coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google uses this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob policies.

After your first completed session, iOS asks whether you allow Fifth Beat to track you across apps. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not shared and ads become less personalized. The game plays exactly the same either way. You can change this any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Where required, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads load, and you can revisit your choices from the Privacy options entry in the app's Settings.

Purchases (Apple)

Remove Ads is a one time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Fifth Beat never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

If you have shared analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send anonymized crash reports to us. They contain no personal information.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or health data. We do not sell personal information. We do not send push notifications. The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of the Kids category.

Your choices

Decline tracking in the iOS prompt or in iOS Settings. Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings. Remove Ads removes interstitial ads entirely. Delete the app to delete all local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
